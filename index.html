Fiverr Gig Stats Tracker

Which gig is actually being seen — and are the numbers going up?

How it works

My Fiverr gigs were getting impressions, but I had no idea which gig was actually being seen — or whether the numbers were going up or down. This tracker logs daily impressions and clicks, shows the best day, compares the gigs at a glance, and keeps the 30-day totals in view. Your log is saved in your browser, so it is still there tomorrow.

Demo example pre-filled below with sample numbers from a real Fiverr account (November 2026). Log your own numbers every day — log either per gig or one account total per day, not both, so nothing is counted twice.
